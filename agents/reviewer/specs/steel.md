# Structural Steel Design Specifications

## 1. Scope

These specifications cover the design, fabrication, and erection of structural steel members and connections for building structures. All work shall conform to applicable codes, standards, and the requirements herein.

## 2. Governing Standards

- **AISC 360**: Specification for Structural Steel Buildings (latest edition)
- **AISC 341**: Seismic Provisions for Structural Steel Buildings (where applicable)
- **AWS D1.1**: Structural Welding Code – Steel
- **ASTM**: Material standards as referenced herein
- **ASCE 7**: Minimum Design Loads and Associated Criteria for Buildings and Other Structures
- **IBC**: International Building Code (current adopted edition)

## 3. Materials

### 3.1 Structural Steel

| Element | ASTM Grade | Min. Fy (ksi) | Min. Fu (ksi) |
|---------|-----------|---------------|---------------|
| Wide-flange shapes (W, S, M, HP) | A992 | 50 | 65 |
| Angles, channels, plates | A36 | 36 | 58 |
| Hollow structural sections (HSS) | A500 Gr. C | 46 (round) / 50 (rect.) | 62 / 62 |
| Steel pipe | A53 Gr. B | 35 | 60 |
| High-strength plates | A572 Gr. 50 | 50 | 65 |

Substitution of a higher-strength grade is permitted only with Engineer of Record (EOR) approval.

### 3.2 Bolts

| Type | ASTM | Application |
|------|------|-------------|
| High-strength hex bolts | A325 / F3125 Gr. A325 | Standard structural connections |
| High-strength hex bolts | A490 / F3125 Gr. A490 | High-load connections (EOR approval required) |
| Anchor rods | F1554 Gr. 36 / 55 / 105 | Column base plates and embedments |

### 3.3 Weld Filler Metals

Weld electrodes and filler metals shall be compatible with the base metal and comply with AWS D1.1 Table 3.1 prequalified joint requirements. Minimum Charpy V-Notch (CVN) toughness of 20 ft-lb at −20 °F is required for demand-critical welds in seismic applications.

### 3.4 Shear Studs

Headed shear studs shall conform to ASTM A108. Minimum 3/4 in. diameter unless otherwise noted on drawings.

## 4. Design Criteria

### 4.1 Design Method

Load and Resistance Factor Design (LRFD) per AISC 360 shall be used unless Allowable Strength Design (ASD) is explicitly specified by the EOR.

### 4.2 Load Combinations

Load combinations shall follow ASCE 7 Section 2.3 (LRFD) or Section 2.4 (ASD). Seismic load combinations shall include the overstrength factor Ω₀ where required by AISC 341.

### 4.3 Deflection Limits

| Member Type | Live Load | Total Load |
|-------------|-----------|------------|
| Floor beams | L / 360 | L / 240 |
| Roof beams (no plaster) | L / 240 | L / 180 |
| Roof beams (with plaster) | L / 360 | L / 240 |
| Cantilevers | L / 180 | L / 120 |
| Crane girders (vertical) | L / 600 | — |

Lateral drift limits shall be per ASCE 7 Table 12.12-1 or as specified by the EOR.

### 4.4 Slenderness Limits

- Compression members: KL/r ≤ 200 (preferred); EOR approval required above 200.
- Tension members: L/r ≤ 300 (preferred).
- Brace members: L/r ≤ 200.

## 5. Connections

### 5.1 General

All connections shall be designed for the forces shown on the structural drawings. Where forces are not shown, connections shall be designed for the lesser of:
- The full strength of the connected member, or
- The maximum force that can be delivered by the system.

Minimum connection capacity: 10 kips shear or 50% of member allowable capacity, whichever is greater.

### 5.2 Bolted Connections

- All high-strength bolts in standard holes shall be installed as **snug-tight** minimum.
- **Slip-critical** classification required for: connections subject to load reversal, fatigue, or where slip would be detrimental.
- **Pre-tensioned** installation required for: anchor rods, moment connections, and connections to column flanges.
- Minimum bolt spacing: 2⅔d (preferred 3d).
- Minimum edge distance: per AISC 360 Table J3.4.

### 5.3 Welded Connections

- Minimum fillet weld size: per AISC 360 Table J2.4.
- Maximum fillet weld size along edges < 1/4 in. thick: equal to material thickness.
- Maximum fillet weld size along edges ≥ 1/4 in. thick: material thickness minus 1/16 in.
- Complete Joint Penetration (CJP) groove welds are required at moment connection beam flanges and at demand-critical locations.
- All CJP welds shall be made with backing bars removed and back-gouged, unless otherwise approved by the EOR.

### 5.4 Base Plates and Anchor Rods

- Base plates shall be designed per AISC Design Guide 1.
- Minimum base plate thickness: 3/4 in.
- Anchor rod projection above grout shall accommodate nut, washer, and leveling nut.
- Grout shall be non-shrink, non-metallic, minimum 5,000 psi compressive strength at 28 days.

## 6. Fabrication

### 6.1 Shop Drawings

Fabricator shall submit shop drawings for EOR review prior to fabrication. Shop drawings shall show:
- Member sizes, lengths, and orientation marks
- Connection details with bolt sizes, grades, quantities, and edge distances
- Weld sizes, types, and lengths
- Camber requirements
- Hole sizes and locations
- Surface preparation and coating system

### 6.2 Tolerances

Fabrication tolerances shall conform to AISC Code of Standard Practice, Section 6. Member length tolerance: ±1/16 in. for members ≤ 30 ft; ±1/8 in. for members > 30 ft.

### 6.3 Camber

Beams with spans ≥ 40 ft carrying significant dead load shall be cambered unless otherwise noted. Camber = 75% of calculated dead-load deflection. Minimum camber: 3/4 in.

### 6.4 Surface Preparation and Coating

| Condition | Requirement |
|-----------|-------------|
| Exposed steel (exterior) | SSPC-SP 6 commercial blast; primer + finish coat per project specifications |
| Concealed steel (interior, dry) | SSPC-SP 3 power-tool clean; one coat shop primer |
| Steel embedded in concrete | No primer; free of oil, grease, loose mill scale |
| Slip-critical faying surfaces | SSPC-SP 6; Class A or B coating if painted |
| Fireproofing substrate | Per fireproofing manufacturer requirements |

## 7. Erection

### 7.1 General

Erection shall conform to AISC Code of Standard Practice, Section 7 and OSHA 29 CFR 1926 Subpart R.

### 7.2 Plumbness and Alignment

- Columns: out-of-plumb ≤ 1/500 of column height, max 1 in. in any 200 ft.
- Beams: elevation tolerance ±3/8 in. from theoretical elevation.
- Twist of members: ≤ 1/8 in. per 10 ft of length.

### 7.3 Temporary Bracing

The erector is responsible for all temporary bracing required to maintain stability during erection. Temporary bracing shall remain in place until the permanent structural system is complete and capable of resisting applied loads.

### 7.4 Field Welding

Field welding is permitted only where indicated on drawings or approved by the EOR. Field welds shall be performed by certified welders under the same quality requirements as shop welds.

## 8. Quality Assurance and Inspection

### 8.1 Fabricator Qualification

Fabricator shall be certified by the AISC Quality Certification Program or demonstrate equivalent quality system to the satisfaction of the EOR.

### 8.2 Special Inspections

Special inspections shall be performed in accordance with IBC Chapter 17 and the project's Statement of Special Inspections. Minimum required inspections:

| Work | Inspection Type |
|------|----------------|
| High-strength bolting (pre-tensioned / slip-critical) | Periodic |
| Complete and partial joint penetration welds | Continuous |
| Fillet welds (demand-critical) | Continuous |
| Fillet welds (non-demand-critical, > 5/16 in.) | Periodic |
| Column base plates and anchor rod installation | Periodic |

### 8.3 Nondestructive Testing (NDT)

| Weld Type | NDT Method | Frequency |
|-----------|-----------|-----------|
| CJP groove welds (tension) | UT or RT | 100% |
| CJP groove welds (compression) | UT or RT | 25% |
| Demand-critical fillet welds | MT or PT | 100% |
| Weld repairs | UT or RT | 100% |

NDT shall be performed by AWS-CWI certified inspectors or ASNT Level II technicians.

### 8.4 Material Certifications

Mill test reports (MTRs) shall be provided for all structural steel and shall conform to the specified ASTM standard. The EOR may require independent testing of any material not accompanied by a compliant MTR.

## 9. Fireproofing

### 9.1 Fire-Resistance Ratings

Fire-resistance ratings shall be as required by IBC Table 601 and as noted on the drawings. Spray-applied fire-resistive material (SFRM) shall comply with UL listings.

### 9.2 Thickness

SFRM thickness shall be determined by the listed assembly and verified by special inspection per IBC Section 1705.13. Minimum average thickness shall not be less than the listed thickness; no single reading shall be less than 80% of the listed thickness.

## 10. Document Compliance Checklist

When reviewing a structural steel design document, verify the following:

- [ ] Governing standards and code edition are cited
- [ ] All steel shapes specify a conforming ASTM grade
- [ ] Design method (LRFD or ASD) is stated
- [ ] Load combinations reference ASCE 7
- [ ] Deflection limits are provided for all beam types
- [ ] Slenderness ratios are within specified limits
- [ ] Connection minimum capacity is satisfied
- [ ] Bolt installation type (snug-tight / pre-tensioned / slip-critical) is specified
- [ ] Weld sizes comply with AISC 360 Table J2.4 minimum and maximum limits
- [ ] CJP welds at moment connections have backing bar removal specified
- [ ] Base plate grout specification is included
- [ ] Camber requirements are addressed for long-span beams
- [ ] Surface preparation class and primer are specified
- [ ] Special inspection types and frequencies are listed
- [ ] NDT requirements are stated for CJP welds
- [ ] Fire-resistance ratings are addressed
